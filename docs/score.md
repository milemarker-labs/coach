# The Marker score

The Marker is a single 0–100 reading of an athlete's fitness, calculated from stored activity summaries. Nothing is generated by a model. The engine is `packages/shared/src/score.ts` (`computeMarker`), so the web app, the public journal and the coach model's context all compute the same number.

The display name is `SCORE_NAME` in that file. Rename it there.

## Inputs

Per activity: local day, sport, suffer score, moving time, distance, average speed and average heart rate. **Load** is the suffer score, or one point per moving minute when there is none (the same rule as the load charts). Up to a year of history warms the averages.

## Parts

| Part | Weight | Reading | 0–100 curve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | 35% | Fitness: 42-day exponentially weighted load (CTL) | `100 × (1 − e^(−CTL/40))` |
| Consistency | 25% | Active days in the last 28, weeks (of 4) with 3+ sessions | `60 × min(1, days/16) + 40 × weeks/4` |
| Balance | 20% | Acute:chronic ratio, ATL ÷ CTL (needs CTL ≥ 3) | 100 for 0.8–1.3; down to 40 at 0.3; down to 0 at 2.0 |
| Momentum | 10% | Change of CTL over 28 days, relative to max(CTL then, 10) | `50 + 125 × change` |
| Efficiency | 10% | Median metres per minute per heartbeat in the main sport (most HR samples, ≥ 2 km), last 42 days vs the 42 before, 3+ samples each | `50 + 500 × change` |

A part with no data is left out and the remaining weights are rescaled. All curves are clamped to 0–100.

**Bands:** Getting started 0+, Building 25+, Steady 45+, Strong 65+, Peak 80+.

**Confidence:** high with 56+ days of history, 16+ active days and every part measurable; medium with 28+ days and 8+ active days; otherwise low.

## Today's call

Evaluated in order. The first match wins.

1. Race in 0–2 days: easy shakeout, 20 minutes.
2. Form (CTL − ATL) below −30, or ratio above 1.5: rest.
3. Form below −15, or ratio above 1.3: easy, 0.75 × typical session.
4. No session in 3+ days: restart, 0.7 × typical session.
5. Weekend, form above −10, and no session of 1.4 × typical length in the last 7 days: long, 1.6 × typical session.
6. Form above +5 and ratio below 1.0: quality, 1 × typical session.
7. Otherwise: steady aerobic work, 1 × typical session.

"Typical session" is the median moving time over the last 28 days (40 minutes when there is none). Durations round to 5 minutes, with a minimum of 15.

## Directions

Up to four, in this order: taper (race within 14 days), ease the ramp (ratio above 1.3) or room to build (ratio below 0.8), add a day (consistency below 60), fading base (momentum below 40), slow the easy days (efficiency below 42), build the engine (base below 40), and one strength to keep (the highest part at 75 or more).

## Where it shows

- Web: dashboard hero, training page, sidebar chip, coach welcome. Computed in the browser from the year of history (`features/score/use-marker.ts`).
- Public journal: `GET /api/journal/mangesh` returns `score` with the number, band, parts and trend. Today's call and the directions stay private.
- Coach model: `buildCoachContext` adds the score, its parts, today's call and the directions to the facts the model sees.

Tests: `apps/web/src/features/analytics/calculations/score.test.ts`.
